---Standardized Romanian Transcription Rules-------------------------------------

FINAL PALATAL CONSONANTSː should be transcribed with a separate segmental jod [j]

VERB FORMSː should not include final -[r e] (or final -[r iː]) or initial [a]- 

DIPHTONGSː 
	e a --> e̯ 'a		e a u --> e̯ 'a w		V i --> 'V j 		
	o a --> o̯ 'a		i a u --> j 'a w		V u --> 'V w

A:
	from Oct 15 onward, treated as +back,-front -- [ɑ], not [a].
		at time of writing, this treatment is favored by at least 32 rules, which require extra specifications to either excluded /a/ from patterning with front vowels, force it to pattern with back vowels, or both. There are only 5 rules [3 between Balkan Romance and Common Romanian, and 2 between Common Romanian and Old Romanian] , that favor a non-back articulation for /a/, and these would largely be satisfied if it was actually low central /ä/ -- but this isn't done at this time.

USE OF Gː g automatically becomes ɡ (Keyman: g<)

GLIDES
u̯ --> w


https://lingv.ro/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/RRL-1-2-2024-Art_12_MARIANA-PREDA_209-219.pdf confirms that vowel hiati are distinct from diphthongs in Romanian.
However, https://archive.org/details/toma-ion-compendiu-de-limba-romana/page/26/mode/2up (translated at https://iit.academiaromana-is.ro/srol/en/hiat.htm ) claims that we will only see vowel hiati in neologisms and loanwords.

For the time being: all orthographic <au> transcribed as /aw/, all orthographic <ie> with stressed /i/ transcribed as /ˈije/, <preot> and <auă> transcribed with hiatus


<ch> AND <gh> (per Pardess pg. 44):
	c h e --> k j e		c h i --> k j i
	g h e --> ɡ j e		g h i --> ɡ j i
UPDATE: Now <chi> and <ghi> are only [kj] and [ɡj] word-finally or before another vowel. When the <i> in this sequence represents a full vowel, we do not transcribe with [j].
